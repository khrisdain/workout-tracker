How the calorie estimate works

Leave the calories field empty and PulseTrack fills it in with the standard MET equation, scaled by how hard the session felt: 

kcal = MET x bodyweight(kg) x hours x intensity factor

Bodyweight comes from your profile. If you have not set one, PulseTrack uses a 70 kg reference, which is another reason to fill in your metrics. Type your own number into the form whenever you have a watch reading you trust more.

A sensible weekly target

The dashboard measures your week against 150 minutes of activity, the figure most public health guidance settles on. It is a floor, not a ceiling, and it counts every type on this page.

The target is configured in application.yml under pulsetrack.workout.weekly-minutes-goal, so an environment can move it without a redeploy of new code.
